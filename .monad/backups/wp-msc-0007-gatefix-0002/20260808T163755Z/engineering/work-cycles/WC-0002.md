## 1. Purpose

WC-0002 defines the trust boundary around compiler observability and safe acceleration. A compilation must not change its normalized meaning merely because it reused prior work, found a cache entry, ran phases in a different permitted order, moved to another workspace path, or repeated under an equivalent environment.

For equivalent normalized inputs, the governing relation is:

```text
clean compilation
  ≡ incremental compilation
  ≡ cached compilation
  ≡ repeated or differently scheduled compilation
```

Equivalence covers normalized semantic output, normalized diagnostics, and output availability. It does not require identical elapsed time, logs, cache-hit layout, timestamps, run identifiers, or other declared operational records. Clean compilation remains the correctness oracle, and reuse is allowed only when compatibility is affirmatively proven.

## 2. Scope

### 2.1 In scope

WC-0002 covers:

- structured, renderer-neutral diagnostics with stable rule and occurrence identities;
- deterministic diagnostic ordering, deduplication, suppression, evidence, and bounded fix proposals;
- normalized compilation requests and complete semantic-input manifests;
- explicit semantic, operational, controlled, and uncontrolled environment classification;
- phase-result contracts, completeness, availability, dependency observations, and reuse declarations;
- dependency discovery, change classification, conservative transitive invalidation, and decision explanations;
- incremental plans that explicitly reuse, recompute, or mark results unavailable;
- clean fallback and cancellation-safe partial-result handling;
- domain-separated cache keys, validated entries, atomic publication, corruption rejection, and eviction transparency;
- schedule-independent aggregation and controlled nondeterminism;
- reproducibility levels, records, comparisons, and optional attestation boundaries;
- security, resource, tenancy, authority, and AI-assistance constraints;
- bootstrap-profile capabilities and honest limitation declarations; and
- differential, property, mutation, fault, security, and matrix conformance obligations.

### 2.2 Out of scope

WC-0002 does not implement or approve:

- MSG graph identity, graph fingerprinting, canonical serialization, validation, or immutability owned by MSC-CORE-0008;
- MKE persistence, indexing, transactions, querying, or cache use as authoritative semantic knowledge;
- KIR lowering, backend-specific diagnostic rules, target availability, backend cache inputs, or generated artifacts;
- compiler-binary identity as a substitute for semantic-result equivalence;
- self-hosting proof governed by MSC-CORE-0010;
- deployment of a remote cache, signature infrastructure, or attestation trust service; or
- the final PI-002 compiler-specification consistency decision.

## 3. Governing invariants

1. Clean compilation is the correctness oracle and remains possible without cache state.
2. Equivalent semantic inputs yield observationally equivalent normalized semantic output, diagnostics, and availability across supported execution modes.
3. Reuse requires affirmative compatibility evidence; unknown state fails safe through recomputation or explicit unavailability.
4. Cache contents are untrusted memoization, never semantic authority or MKE knowledge.
5. Diagnostic rule, occurrence, set, manifest, run, graph, and fingerprint identities are distinct and domain-separated.
6. Stable diagnostic identity is independent of renderer, locale, machine-local absolute path, and permitted schedule.
7. Every semantic input is normalized and captured, or the resulting reproducibility claim is honestly limited.
8. Hidden or unobservable dependencies prevent reuse.
9. Deletion, rename, policy, plugin, configuration, and unknown changes cannot leave stale semantic results or diagnostics.
10. Cancellation, corruption, partial writes, and incompatible state never publish reusable work.
11. Concurrency may affect performance but not normalized meaning.
12. Diagnostic-policy-only changes may alter findings without falsely implying changed MSG semantic content.
13. Reproducibility claims state the comparison level and evidence actually established; attestations confer no independent semantic authority.
14. AI may explain, classify, or propose, but may not suppress findings, accept results, mutate authority, or assert reproducibility without governed authorization.
15. Architecture remains frozen by default and changes only through constitutional governance.

## 6. Dependency and ownership gates

The review confirmed that:

- every MSC-CORE-0009 implementation area has one primary packet owner;
- packet dependencies are acyclic and permit the two foundation packets to begin independently;
- manifests declare normalized inputs, dependency tracking observes effects, planning decides work, caching validates stored results, reproducibility compares outcomes, and conformance attempts to falsify the full chain;
- diagnostic identity and rendering remain separate;
- manifest identity, cache keys, diagnostic-set identity, MSG graph identity, and MSG fingerprint remain distinct;
- diagnostic-only policy changes can invalidate findings without inventing an MSG content change;
- cache validation never transfers semantic authority to cache state;
- reused pre-MSG state cannot bypass MSC-CORE-0008 validation, canonicalization, or immutability gates; and
- MSC-CORE-0010 retains target diagnostics, backend compatibility inputs, and self-hosting evidence.

Changes to this ownership model require review against MSC-CORE-0009 and the final PI-002 cross-specification contract.
