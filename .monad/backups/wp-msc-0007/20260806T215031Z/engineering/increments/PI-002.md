PI-002 defines the remaining foundation of the Monad Specification Compiler after the upstream compiler contracts. It must establish the canonical semantic graph, compiler-wide diagnostics and reproducibility behavior, and the lowering/backend/self-hosting boundary as one internally consistent specification set.

The increment is not complete merely because WC-0001 passed. MSC-CORE-0009 and MSC-CORE-0010 remain open, the combined MSC-CORE-0008–0010 consistency review has not occurred, and no compiler implementation threshold has been declared.

## Increment objectives

1. Specify deterministic construction of one immutable Monad Semantic Graph from frozen semantic-analysis results.
2. Specify diagnostics, incremental compilation, caching, invalidation, and reproducibility across the compiler lifecycle.
3. Specify KIR lowering, backend contracts, target support, and the self-hosting path without weakening canonical MSG semantics.
4. Reconcile MSC-CORE-0008 through MSC-CORE-0010 into one coherent compiler contract.
5. Declare the implementation threshold only after the specification set passes its consistency review.

## WC-0001 accepted results

WC-0001 established that:

- MSG is the canonical semantic output of one MSC compilation snapshot;
- MSG is distinct from AST/compiler-private state, MKE persistence, and KIR lowering;
- semantic identity is distinct from graph-local member identity;
- graph identity is distinct from canonical-content fingerprint;
- provenance, authority, lifecycle, applicability, evidence, and uncertainty are semantic data;
- incomplete and conflicting knowledge must be preserved or diagnosed rather than silently removed;
- readiness is output-specific;
- published snapshots are deterministic and deeply immutable; and
- the MKE boundary ends in a governed ingestion handoff, not compiler ownership of persistence.

The WC-0001 construction review found no unresolved P0 or P1 planning blocker and required no Architecture Freeze amendment.

## Cross-cycle reconciliation obligations

### WC-0002 / MSC-CORE-0009

WC-0002 must define the compiler-wide policy surrounding deterministic inputs, diagnostics, incremental invalidation, caching, repeatable construction, and reproducibility records. It must extend rather than contradict the deterministic snapshot requirements already accepted in MSC-CORE-0008.

### WC-0003 / MSC-CORE-0010

WC-0003 must preserve the one-way boundary:

```text
MSG → KIR → backends and target artifacts
```

Target-specific constraints may affect lowering and output availability but must not redefine canonical graph meaning. Self-hosting evidence must remain anchored to stable MSG semantics and governed reproducibility.

### Final consistency review

The final review must explicitly reconcile:

- terminology and identifiers shared across MSC-CORE-0008–0010;
- construction determinism versus compiler-wide reproducibility;
- diagnostics associated with partial graphs and consumer-specific readiness;
- cache keys, fingerprints, graph identity, lineage, and invalidation;
- MSG completeness versus KIR/backend target support;
- canonical serialization and conformance expectations; and
- bootstrap and self-hosting obligations.
