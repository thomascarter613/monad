WC-0001 is **planning-complete**. The cycle established the normative semantic-graph construction contract, decomposed that contract into six ordered implementation work packets, published its engineering-journal explanation, and passed its construction review.

Planning completion is not implementation completion. No production MSG compiler, MKE persistence adapter, KIR lowering pipeline, canonical serialization implementation, or self-hosting capability is claimed by this record. MSC-CORE-0008 is accepted within PI-002 subject to reconciliation with MSC-CORE-0009 and MSC-CORE-0010 during the compiler-specification consistency review required before the compiler implementation threshold is declared.

## 1. Purpose

WC-0001 specifies and decomposes the transformation of frozen, analyzed compiler knowledge into one deterministic, immutable Monad Semantic Graph snapshot.

The work cycle closes the canonical boundary between semantic analysis and downstream persistence, lowering, publication, search, applications, and AI-context assembly:

```text
MSL
  ↓
MSC
  ↓
MSG
  ↓
MKE
  ↓
Projections, KIR, Backends, Applications, and AI Context
```

The cycle ensures that downstream systems consume one governed semantic result rather than reconstructing meaning independently from compiler-private state.

## 2. Scope

### 2.1 In scope

WC-0001 covers:

- the frozen semantic-analysis input boundary;
- the canonical MSG value model;
- graph roots, semantic entities, relationships, and claims;
- typed values and relationship reification;
- durable semantic identity and graph-local identity;
- provenance, source lineage, authority, lifecycle, and applicability;
- evidence, uncertainty, incomplete knowledge, conflicts, and external references;
- graph profiles, extensions, invariants, completeness, and output readiness;
- deterministic member identity assignment and canonical ordering;
- graph identity, content fingerprinting, and parent lineage;
- deep immutability, canonical JSON, and serialization round trips;
- construction and reproducibility records;
- the MKE ingestion handoff boundary; and
- implementation decomposition and conformance obligations.

### 2.2 Out of scope

WC-0001 does not implement or approve:

- parser, AST, name-resolution, type-analysis, or other upstream compiler behavior;
- an MKE database schema, persistence engine, transaction model, or query implementation;
- KIR lowering, backend contracts, or generated target artifacts;
- publication-site or AI-context application implementation;
- compiler-wide diagnostics, incrementality, caching, or reproducibility policy governed by MSC-CORE-0009;
- KIR, backend, and self-hosting requirements governed by MSC-CORE-0010; or
- the final PI-002 compiler-specification consistency decision.

## 3. Governing invariants

The cycle preserves the following architectural constraints:

1. MSG is the canonical semantic output of one MSC compilation snapshot.
2. MSG is not an AST, symbol table, graph-database schema, or KIR.
3. Compiler-only mutable state does not enter the published MSG contract.
4. MKE persistence remains downstream of MSG construction.
5. Target-specific lowering remains downstream in KIR and backend contracts.
6. Semantic identity is distinct from graph-local member identity.
7. Graph identity is distinct from its canonical-content fingerprint.
8. Provenance, authority, lifecycle, applicability, evidence, and uncertainty are semantic data, not presentation metadata.
9. Incomplete, unresolved, ambiguous, external, invalid, unsupported, and conflicting knowledge is preserved or diagnosed rather than silently discarded.
10. A partial graph may be ready for selected outputs without being universally ready.
11. Published snapshots are deterministic and deeply immutable; changed meaning produces a new snapshot with lineage.
12. AI may assist or propose but has no independent acceptance authority.
13. Architecture remains frozen by default and changes only through constitutional governance.

## 6. Dependency and ownership gates

The construction review confirmed that:

- every MSC-CORE-0008 responsibility has a primary work-packet owner;
- the six packets form a valid producer-consumer sequence;
- apparent overlaps are interface handoffs rather than duplicated ownership;
- WP-MSC-0004 assigns semantic and graph-local member identities, while WP-MSC-0006 finalizes graph identity and computes its fingerprint;
- WP-MSC-0005 determines completeness and output readiness, while WP-MSC-0006 records those results in the immutable snapshot; and
- WP-MSC-0006 ends at an MKE ingestion handoff and does not assume MKE persistence ownership.

Changes to this ownership model require review against MSC-CORE-0008 and the accepted architectural spine.
