# WP-AF-0002 — Define Monad Engineering Principles

## Executive Summary

Establish the enduring engineering principles that govern the design, implementation, and evolution of the Monad ecosystem.

Where the Monad Manifesto explains **why Monad exists**, the Engineering Principles define **how Monad should be engineered**. These principles provide stable guidance for architectural decisions, specification authoring, implementation, documentation, and long-term maintenance.

Unlike implementation details, these principles are expected to remain valid across multiple major releases.

---

# Problem Statement

Large engineering projects gradually accumulate inconsistent assumptions.

Different contributors optimize for different goals.

Over time this results in:

* architectural drift;
* duplicated concepts;
* inconsistent implementations;
* conflicting specifications;
* reduced maintainability.

Monad requires a stable set of engineering principles against which every architectural decision can be evaluated.

---

# Motivation

The Engineering Principles provide a common engineering philosophy shared by every subsystem.

They establish:

* engineering priorities;
* design trade-offs;
* architectural consistency;
* implementation expectations;
* long-term maintainability goals.

Every future specification should be explainable in terms of these principles.

---

# Objectives

This work packet establishes:

1. Engineering knowledge as the primary artifact.
2. Knowledge compilation as the preferred engineering model.
3. Preservation of semantic meaning.
4. Explicit provenance.
5. Stable identity.
6. Canonical knowledge.
7. Deterministic compilation.
8. Layered responsibility.
9. Separation of knowledge from presentation.
10. Long-term architectural consistency.

# Engineering Notes

The Engineering Principles translate the philosophical direction established by the Monad Manifesto into actionable engineering guidance.

While the Manifesto explains *why* Monad exists, the Principles provide a stable framework for evaluating future design and implementation decisions.

These principles are intentionally technology-agnostic and are expected to remain valid as Monad evolves.
