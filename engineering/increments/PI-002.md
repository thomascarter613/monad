## Increment objectives

1. Specify deterministic construction of one immutable Monad Semantic Graph from frozen semantic-analysis results.
2. Specify diagnostics, incremental compilation, caching, invalidation, and reproducibility across the compiler lifecycle.
3. Specify KIR lowering, backend contracts, target support, artifact publication, and bounded self-hosting evidence without weakening MSG authority.
4. Reconcile MSC-CORE-0008–0010 into one coherent compiler contract.
5. Declare the implementation threshold only after the specification set passes its governing reviews.

## Cross-specification obligations

The final review must verify at minimum:

- consistent terminology, phase ownership, and one-way authority flow;
- separation of AST/compiler-private state, MSG, MKE, KIR, backend state, caches, and generated artifacts;
- distinct identity and fingerprint domains;
- complete provenance and diagnostic flow across representation boundaries;
- consistent treatment of partiality, readiness, loss, and unsupported targets;
- sound dependency observation, invalidation, reuse, and reproducibility contracts;
- deterministic MSG-to-KIR lowering and least-authority backend behavior;
- candidate validation, collision safety, atomic publication, and rollback;
- bounded bootstrap/self-hosting comparisons and non-self-promotion; and
- classification and governance of every open question and finding.
