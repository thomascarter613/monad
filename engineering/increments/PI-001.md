## Accepted Outcomes

PI-001 established:

- engineering knowledge as the primary artifact;
- MSL → MSC → MSG → MKE as the core architectural spine;
- distinct boundaries among compiler state, semantic knowledge, persistence, lowering, and publication;
- local-first deterministic core operation;
- explicit identity, provenance, authority, lifecycle, evidence, conflict, and history;
- Work Packets as canonical units of engineering work;
- constitutional governance for architectural change;
- AI as assistant and proposer rather than acceptance authority;
- staged self-hosting without privileged governance bypass.

## Deferred Work

The following were intentionally deferred:

- detailed MSG construction semantics;
- diagnostics and incremental-compilation contracts;
- KIR lowering and backend contracts;
- concrete MKE persistence schema;
- implementation package layout;
- production compiler code;
- complete self-hosting.

These items belong to PI-002 and later increments.
